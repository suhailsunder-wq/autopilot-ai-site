Example workflow

Simple enough to follow.
Human-led by design.

The current working prototype combines a web form with local automation, AI analysis and Telegram alerts. The demonstration uses fictional data.

A customer submits feedback

A short form collects a rating and a comment. The simulated demonstration uses invented examples, not real salon customer records.

AI organises the message

The workflow categorises feedback and helps identify comments that may need attention. AI classifications can be wrong and should be reviewed.

The owner receives an alert

An alert highlights an issue, such as a long wait or dissatisfaction. The system does not automatically contact the customer.

A team member decides what happens next

Staff review the issue and decide whether and how to respond using their existing complaint-handling process.

Pilot limitations

Demo first. Real data later.

Feedback Manager Lite is currently an early pilot hosted on a local computer. It is not marketed as a continuously available production service. Before any real customer information is processed, we need suitable agreements, security safeguards and reliable hosting.

Frequently asked

What the pilot does—and doesn't do.

Can I connect real salon customer information today?

No. The current demonstration is limited to fictional examples. Production data processing requires additional operational, privacy and security readiness.

Will the system automatically reply to unhappy clients?

No. It categorises feedback and notifies the owner for human review.

Is there a monthly plan available?

Not currently. This page invites requests for a simulated demonstration. Commercial terms will only be discussed once the service is ready to offer.
