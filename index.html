Questions answered

Before you begin.

Are the digital products available to buy now?

Yes. The free calculator and $19 toolkit are listed on Gumroad, where purchases, payment processing and file delivery take place.

Does the calculator tell me exactly what clients will pay?

No. It estimates what you may need to charge based on your own inputs. Market demand, skill level, your positioning, and local taxes can change the price you actually quote.

Does Feedback Manager automatically reply to customers?

No. The early pilot categorises feedback and flags comments for a business owner to review. It does not respond to customers or guarantee round-the-clock operation.
